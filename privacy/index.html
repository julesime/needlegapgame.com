Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

Overview

Needle Gap Reflex is developed by Julien Simiand. The app is a minimal arcade/reflex game and does not require an app-specific account.

Data Collection

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

The developer does not collect personal data from Needle Gap Reflex.

  • No developer analytics are used.
  • No ads are shown.
  • No third-party tracking SDKs are included.
  • No app-specific account is required.

Local Device Storage

Needle Gap Reflex may store gameplay progress, scores, preferences, or settings locally on your device. This local information is not collected by the developer.

Apple Game Center

Apple Game Center is optional and may be used for leaderboard features. Game Center data is handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

You can choose not to sign in to Game Center or disable Game Center in iOS settings.
